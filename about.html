Mission

To spark curiosity, encourage exploration and inspire students to look beyond the classroom
and discover the wonders of the universe.

About the Astronomy Club @ PSG iTech

✦ A community of students passionate about astronomy, astrophysics and space.

✦ A platform to explore and learn about the universe beyond the classroom.

✦ Encourages curiosity, exploration and scientific thinking.

✦ Provides opportunities for practical learning and observation.

✦ Promotes teamwork, creativity and knowledge sharing.

✦ A place to observe, explore, learn and look beyond the ordinary.

Objectives of the Club

✦ Build a community of students passionate about exploring the universe.

✦ Nurture interest in astronomy and related technologies.

✦ Promote science communication and astronomy outreach.

✦ Provide practical exposure to astronomical observation.

✦ Provide hands-on experience with astronomical instruments.

✦ Encourage scientific thinking and interdisciplinary learning.

✦ Develop skills in observation, astrophotography, data analysis and teamwork.

✦ Encourage participation in astronomy and science-related activities.
